Novel

I have a whole Obsidian Vault filled with ideas that vaguely fit together and world building and character concepts that are varying levels of compatible with each other.

The main story I hope to turn into a written novel instead of another type of media is about a young god who was forced to kill her father, the former god of the land, who succumbed to a deadly corruption that drove him mad. But the energy released upon his death petrified the land. Now she must find a way to undo the curse, traveling the world and learning what it was that her father saw in humanity to protect them so. And maybe fall in love with a mortal maiden along the way :D

It's dressed as a comming of age story and a romance, but it's actually about the indifference of power and the significance of care.
